Liquid made of light & math

A program moves thousands of tiny spheres every fraction of a second. Together they squish, flow, and pile up so your eye reads them as one soft, splashy mass—like gel beads in a clear box.

  • The glowing blob is the simulated fluid—each dot is a particle.
  • The wireframe cube is the glass tank that holds it.
  • You steer it with a mouse or trackpad—or let the camera follow your hand.

No install. Works on laptop, tablet, and phone.

You’re watching a real-time physics toy: many small spheres pooled in a box, blended to look and move like soft liquid.

How to play · what the numbers mean

Controls: drag on the scene to tilt the tank. Shift+drag moves it, Ctrl/Cmd+drag pushes depth, scroll wheel zooms. Alt+drag stirs the fluid.

FPS
How often the picture updates—higher feels smoother.
N
How many particles—more dots, richer “liquid,” heavier work for your device.
MODE
Mouse/trackpad vs. camera hand tracking.
SIG
What the sim thinks you’re doing (hold, stir, pointer).
